\section{Question and scope}
Which guide format did visitors choose during the example session?
The observations are fictional.

\section{Findings}
Two of the three visitors chose print. One chose digital \cite{demo-observations}.

\section{Limitations}
One session gives no basis for estimating preferences across the library's visitors.

\section{Open question}
Do choices vary by time of day or by whether a visitor has a device on hand?
